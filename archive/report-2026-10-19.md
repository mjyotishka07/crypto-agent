## How to read this
- **ROI**: how much the price changed over that period.
- **Volatility**: how wildly the price swings. Higher means riskier.
- **Worst drop**: biggest fall from a peak in the past year.
- **Sharpe / Sortino**: return per unit of risk. Above 1 is good, below 0 is poor.
- **Beta vs BTC**: above 1 means it moves more than Bitcoin, below 1 less.
- **RSI**: above 70 often means overheated, below 30 oversold.
- **Trend**: simple reading from moving averages, 30-day return and RSI.
- **Score**: relative ranking among these coins only, not a buy signal.

Data: CoinGecko, alternative.me. Past performance does not predict future results.
